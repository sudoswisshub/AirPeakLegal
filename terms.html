3. Environmental Data & Modeling Limitations

AirPeak aggregates official open government measurement feeds provided by Swiss federal authorities:

  • MeteoSwiss Automatic Pollen Monitoring Network: Real grains per cubic metre updated hourly from active automated monitoring stations.
  • FOEN / BAFU NABEL Network: Swiss National Air Pollution Monitoring Network measuring ozone, nitrogen dioxide, and particulate matter.
  • Swisstopo Height & Elevation APIs: Elevation-aware profile estimations.

Measurements may experience delays, maintenance outages, or preliminary status. High-altitude concentrations, relief destination forecasts, and regional interpolations are mathematical estimates derived from baseline station readings and labeled as modeled rather than direct measurements.
